---
name: Homepage PWA scope
description: Keep the initial PWA limited to home installation, without offline private or financial data.
---
تفعيل PWA مخصص للشاشة الرئيسية فقط، ولا يشمل تطوير الأقسام الأخرى.

**Why:** طلب المستخدم حصر العمل في إنشاء manifest.json وتفعيل PWA للشاشة الرئيسية حصرياً.

**How to apply:** Keep the installed entry point at home. Do not extend this feature into dashboard, reports or booking workflows without a new request. The initial offline experience must show an honest connection-required message, not cached account data, invented availability, automatic payment retries or page reloads that discard forms.
