---
name: General activity expenses
description: Expense entry must not require a second activity selection; lone-activity labels can conceal an all scope.
---
Allow direct expense entry without requiring a property/service selection. Use the currently selected activity automatically; when there is only one activity, use that activity even if the dashboard's internal scope is still all. A genuinely consolidated multi-activity view permits general expenses and optional allocation.

**Why:** The user reported being forced to select a property or service despite their business already appearing at the top. The single-activity selector displays its name without changing an internal all scope.

**How to apply:** Resolve the expense target from both scope and available activities, not the visible selector label alone. Keep general expenses distinct from provider-specific expenses; do not distribute a general amount across unrelated providers.
