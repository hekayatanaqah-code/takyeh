---
name: Photography delivery integrity
description: Final artifacts, competencies and immutable delivery versus historical legal consent
---
Delivery means final photos/files or an HTTPS download link, not merely clicking “completed” after the appointment. Notify the owner once and retain photographer access to historical deliveries.

**Why:** The user requested actual deliverables and owner review, with preserved photographer history.

**How to apply:** Validate assignment and session completion atomically with the first delivery, and treat retries as acknowledgements of that original delivery. Copy uploaded objects to private immutable destinations pinned to the verified storage generation; a still-valid signed PUT must not overwrite delivered artifacts.

Administrative package/specialty approvals are current operational qualifications, not edits to historically signed legal terms.

**Why:** The user requested competencies in the profile and contract area; that is not permission to rewrite past contractual consent.

**How to apply:** Show current approvals separately from signed terms, and require approved package, compatible scope and city for new assignments.
